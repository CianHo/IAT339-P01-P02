Who We Are

We here at Latimports are dedicated to bringing the highest quality ingredients and foods from Latim America to Canada! From Adobo to Zacahuil, we source the finest products and deliver them direct to you. Founded in 1989, Latimports has helped foster the food service industry in Canada for decades. Over the years we have remained committed to supplying local businesses with high quality goods at fair prices, because nothing is more important to use than supporting the communities we live in.
