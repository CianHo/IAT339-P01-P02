LatImports announces New CFO, Penelope Pyncher

We are proud to announce that LatImports has a new CFO, Penelope Pyncher. Penelope is replacing our former CFO, Dean Narrow, who retired in September. Penelope has been with LatImports for 10 years, initially starting as an intern. She has been the Accounting Department's Employee of the Month 100 times, setting a record that has yet to be beaten. We are looking forward to having Penelope at the helm as we continue to navigate the current economic landscape.
